Our approach

Your day doesn't
have to be perfect.

The Gentle Day is a small printable collection built around a simple idea: planning can leave room for real life.

The pages are quiet, clear, and unhurried. Use a worksheet to find your next small step, then make the rest of the day your own.

A few helpful details

Before you begin.

Can I buy or download a printable now?

Not yet. The first edition PDFs have been prepared and the images show actual pages. Checkout, customer support details, and purchase policies still need to be completed before sales open.

What do the prices include?

Each individual product includes the stated number of worksheet designs in two separate PDFs, one A4 and one US Letter, plus a separate one-page printing guide. These are the same designs in two sizes, not additional unique worksheets. All displayed prices are in US dollars; any applicable taxes will need to be shown in the future checkout.

Will a physical item be shipped?

No. These are printable digital PDFs. You supply the paper, printer, and pen. Delivery through checkout will be confirmed before purchases become available.

Can I type into these files?

The current edition is designed to print and fill in by hand. It does not contain interactive form fields or planner-app hyperlinks.

What paper sizes are included?

A4 (210 × 297 mm) and US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches), in portrait orientation. Choose the matching file for your printer. Pages have white backgrounds, fine rules, and light sage section headings.

Where can I find contact and purchase policies?

See our contact, terms, privacy, and refunds pages for the current launch status. Verified seller and support information and final policies must be added before accepting orders.
