Proposed 14-day voluntary refund policy

For purchases made after the shop opens, the proposed policy allows a buyer to request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase, including when they change their mind. This voluntary proposal is awaiting owner approval and is not an active sales offer.

How a request would work

Email limiriagonto@gmail.com with the order reference, purchase date, product name, and the email used at checkout. Please do not send full card details, passwords, identity documents, or sensitive journal entries.

Incorrect, missing, or unusable files

Contact support if you receive the wrong file, cannot access the download, encounter a duplicate charge, or find a material issue with the product. The proposed approach is to help restore access, provide the correct file, or arrange a refund as appropriate. Mandatory rights for faulty or misdescribed products are unaffected by the voluntary 14-day period.

Payment provider and processing

No checkout is active. The refund channel, original-payment-method handling, and processing arrangements must be finalized with the chosen provider before sales open. No payment-provider approval or fixed bank-processing timeframe is promised.

Digital delivery and consumer rights

No blanket “no refunds on digital products” rule is applied. Any applicable statutory cancellation, withdrawal, or refund rights take priority. If the future checkout requires consent to immediate digital delivery, the required information and consent must be presented there before payment.
